Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

French Vocab Drill respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices when using the app.

Information We Collect

French Vocab Drill does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal information.

Your learning progress and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your study data.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

French Vocab Drill offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (billed monthly, every three months, or yearly) that unlock all vocabulary, conjugation, and listening modules. Customers who previously made the earlier one-time unlock purchase keep full access at no additional cost.

Payments are securely processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

To manage subscriptions and determine what content you are entitled to, we use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service that processes purchase information on our behalf. The information handled for this purpose includes:

  • An anonymous app user identifier
  • Purchase and subscription status
  • Product purchased
  • Basic device and app information necessary to provide purchase services

This information is not linked to your name or contact details.

Analytics and Advertising

French Vocab Drill does not display advertisements.

The current version does not use third-party analytics services and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Purchase and subscription information is shared only as necessary with Apple and with RevenueCat to provide and manage in-app purchase functionality. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

Children's Privacy

French Vocab Drill does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
